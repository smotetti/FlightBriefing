Import button

Set up once

  1. Check that the address below is the page that opens your flight briefing (tap Open it to test). Then tap Copy code.
  2. In Safari tap Share, then Add Bookmark. Name it Briefing, choose Favorites and save. This only saves this page for now.
  3. Open your bookmarks (the book icon), choose Favorites, tap Edit and tap Briefing. Delete the address and paste the code you copied. Tap Done.

Open it to test

Use it on every flight

  1. Log into the portal and open the duty plan page (the list of your days and flights). For the logbook, open the monthly roster page instead.
  2. Tap the address bar and tap Briefing in your Favorites.
  3. Wait while it collects every flight of today. The flight briefing page then opens with all the cards.
  4. You can also tap Briefing on a single flight's Details page to add just that flight.
